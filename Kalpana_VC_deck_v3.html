Why We Can Price What Others Can’t

OpenAI charges per token because their cost is per token. Kalpanā can offer flat-rate pricing because our compute cost is bounded — regardless of conversation length.

Their cost curve

Every follow-up re-processes the entire conversation history. Cost grows O(N²) over a session.

→ Must charge per token. Can’t offer flat rate without losing money on heavy users.

Our cost curve

Each follow-up processes only new tokens. RIF state holds history. Cost stays O(N) — linear in new tokens only.

→ Flat subscription is profitable. Heavy users cost the same as light users.
